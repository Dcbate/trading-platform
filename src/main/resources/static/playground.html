＋Open account

Creates a client's bank account. Type doesn't change behavior in this build — it's a label (FX_TRADING is what funds FX orders).

🔍Look up accounts

Fetch one account by id, or list everything a client owns.

↓Deposit

Add money to an account.

↑Withdraw

Take money out of an account (fails with 409 if it would go negative).

⇄Transfer to another client

Pay another client at this bank — instant, same-currency, no saga needed.

💱Convert currency

"Sell balance" — move money between two of the same client's own accounts at the live FX rate. Needs at least one price tick to have published (wait a couple of seconds after startup).

🌍Pay another bank

Cross-border payment: fraud check → settlement saga → simulated bank clearing. Amounts over 500,000 deterministically fail clearing (test seam) so you can see the compensation path.

💰Take out a loan

Pick a loan product — rate and term come from the product, not typed in. Principal is credited straight into the account. Interest accrues daily on a schedule — you won't see it move within this session, but the endpoint below shows the current accrued amount.

📄View / repay a loan

Repayments apply to accrued interest first, then principal.

📈FX trading desk — place order

Submit a buy or sell on a currency pair. Post a SELL then a matching BUY at the same price/quantity to watch them fill each other.
